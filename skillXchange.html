How it works

A simple loop for real learning.

SkillSwap is built on reciprocity. You don't pay to learn — you trade. Every minute you teach earns credits you can spend on any skill, from anyone, anywhere. The system handles matching, scheduling, and trust so you can focus on what matters: growing together.

Credit economy

Time is the only currency that matters.

Skill Credits represent time contributed to the community. Earn them by helping others. Spend them to learn from anyone. No money. No speculation. Just reciprocity.

1 teaching minute = 1 credit
A 60-minute session earns 60 credits. Configurable by community admins.
Ledger-based & transactional
Every credit movement is recorded, idempotent, and reversible in disputes.

Trust & safety

Built on reputation, not assumptions.

Every member builds a track record. Skills get verified through evidence, assessments, and peer endorsements. Every transaction is logged, every review is moderated, every signal matters.

Multi-layer verification

Self-reported, community-verified, evidence-verified, assessment-verified, and organization-verified levels — each clearly displayed.

Evidence portfolios

Upload certificates, projects, portfolios, and peer endorsements. Admins review with full audit history.

Multi-dimensional ratings

Communication, knowledge, reliability, and would-recommend. Not a single vanity number.

Anti-gaming protection

Detects fake review loops, suspicious transaction patterns, multi-account abuse — escalated to humans, never auto-punished.

Privacy by design

Granular visibility controls. Profile, email, location, availability, online status — all configurable by you.

Badge system

Earn badges through real milestones — First Session, Top Mentor, Reliable Partner, Verified Skill, Community Builder.

Everything included

A complete platform, not a feature list.

Real-time chat

WebSocket-powered messaging with typing indicators, read receipts, and file sharing — but only after a match is accepted.

Video sessions

Provider-agnostic video meetings. Daily, LiveKit, Whereby — your choice. Manual links supported as fallback.

Smart scheduling

Timezone-aware calendar with availability overlap, ICS export, reminders, and reschedule workflows.

Resource library

Share articles, videos, courses, GitHub repos, and templates. Tag by skill. Visibility controls per resource.

Semantic search

"Photoshop" finds Adobe Photoshop, Graphic Design, and Photo Editing. Powered by embeddings plus structured filters.

Notifications

In-app, email, and push-ready. Granular preferences per category. Never spammy by default.

Group learning

Host sessions with multiple learners. Credits split automatically based on configurable rules.

Analytics dashboard

Track activation, retention, session completion, credit circulation, and marketplace balance.
